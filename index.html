What does "evidence-based" mean? These trainings have been studied by experts who published their findings in peer-reviewed journals — meaning other experts checked the research before it was published. This helps us feel confident the training works. Local/emerging trainings haven't been formally studied yet, but that doesn't mean they aren't helpful — many make a real difference and are available right here in Greater Cincinnati.
